Audio-driven emotional talking face generation aims to synthesize videos where facial expressions are dynamically synchronized with speech content and emotional states. Existing methods struggle to balance controllability and visual fidelity: implicit representation-based approaches can capture rich semantics but lack structural guidance, leading to unstable or entangled emotional dynamics, while explicit geometric-based methods offer better control but often sacrifice high-frequency texture details, resulting in degraded photorealism. To address this dilemma, we propose GemTalk, a diffusion-based framework that unifies the strengths of both paradigms through geometry-guided emotion modulation. Specifically, we design a Vision-guided Audio Emotion Projection (V-AEP) module to extract emotion-discriminative and lip-synchronized implicit features from audio, guided by visual emotional cues during training. In parallel, a Diffusion-based Geometric Priors Generator (D-GPG) synthesizes identity-aware blendshape coefficients conditioned on both audio and the reference identity, providing explicit and interpretable structural priors. These priors are subsequently leveraged by our core Geometry-guided Emotion Modulation (GEM) module, which recalibrates the magnitude of implicit emotional features to achieve fine-grained and continuous control over emotion intensity, while preserving the photorealism benefits of implicit representations. Extensive experiments demonstrate that GemTalk achieves superior performance in photo-realism, and facial emotional dynamics compared with existing state-of-the-art methods, while enabling flexible and continuous emotion intensity control.
